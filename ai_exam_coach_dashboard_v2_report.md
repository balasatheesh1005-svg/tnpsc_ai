# Flagship AI Exam Coach Dashboard V2 Report

## 1. Executive Summary
Sprint 6 delivers the flagship **AI Exam Coach Dashboard V2** for TNPSC Nova AI. Unlike previous sprints that constructed specialized core intelligence engines, Sprint 6 builds the unified, intelligent landing experience that brings all 8 completed core AI engines together: Learning Intelligence V2, Study Planner V2, Recommendation Engine V2, Exam Readiness Engine V2, Mock Exam Intelligence V2, Predictive Performance Engine V2, Adaptive Final Revision Engine V2, and Exam Strategy Engine V2.

Crucially, the dashboard is a **pure presentation layer**. It contains **zero calculation, prediction, evaluation, or strategy creation logic**, and renders outputs generated exclusively by existing master core engines.

## 2. Dashboard Architecture
The AI Exam Coach Dashboard V2 sits as the single unified presentation layer atop all 8 completed intelligence engines:

```
[ Learning Intelligence V2 ] ──┐
[ Study Planner V2 ]         ──┤
[ Recommendation Engine V2 ] ──┤
[ Exam Readiness V2 ]        ──┼─► [ AI Exam Coach Dashboard V2 ]
[ Mock Intelligence V2 ]     ──┤   (Pure Presentation Layer)
[ Predictive Engine V2 ]     ──┤
[ Adaptive Revision V2 ]     ──┤
[ Exam Strategy Engine V2 ]  ──┘
```

The dashboard serves as:
- Primary home screen landing experience for TNPSC Nova AI
- Single answer source for all 7 student questions:
  1. *Where am I now?* (Exam Readiness & Current Status)
  2. *What should I study today?* (Study Planner & Recommendation)
  3. *Am I improving?* (Predictive Trajectory & Learning Insights)
  4. *What are my weak areas?* (Weakness & Learning Intelligence)
  5. *What should I revise?* (Adaptive Final Revision)
  6. *What is my exam strategy?* (Exam Strategy Engine)
  7. *What should I do next?* (Single Next Best Action)

## 4. Dashboard Sections
The dashboard presents 10 structured sections + Hero + Quick Actions:
- **Hero Section**: Student Name, Current Goal, Overall Readiness Score & Level, Today's Focus, Streak, Quick Greeting.
- **Section 1 (Current Status)**: Overall Readiness, Readiness Level, Study Consistency %, Mastered & Pending Topic counts, Streak.
- **Section 2 (Today's Plan)**: Study Plan, Revision Target, Practice Task, Recommended Mock, Estimated Time.
- **Section 3 (Learning Insights)**: Strong & Weak Subjects, Improving & Declining Topics, Trajectory.
- **Section 4 (Mock Insights)**: Latest Mock Score, Accuracy %, Time per question, Mistake Patterns.
- **Section 5 (Exam Readiness)**: Overall Readiness, Subject Breakdown, 5-Dimension Indicators.
- **Section 6 (Prediction)**: Estimated Improvement Range, Prediction Confidence %, Learning Trajectory (Prohibits guaranteed claims).
- **Section 7 (Revision)**: Today's Revision Target, Priority Subjects, Active Revision Cycle, Completion Progress %.
- **Section 8 (Exam Strategy)**: Execution Theme, Subject Attempt Flow, Time Allocation Summary, Risk Alerts, Mentor Strategy.
- **Section 9 (Next Best Action)**: ONE single primary action card from Recommendation Engine V2.
- **Section 10 (AI Mentor Summary)**: Unified, concise pre-exam mentor summary.
- **Quick Actions Bar**: Direct action buttons to Study Plan, Revision, Mocks, Weak Topics, PYQs, and Notes.

## 5. Hero Experience
The Hero Section introduces a high-impact glassmorphic banner displaying the student's name, current streak, overall readiness percentage, readiness classification stage, and today's primary focus recommendation. It sets an encouraging, high-performance tone immediately upon opening the app.

## 6. Next Best Action Logic
To avoid decision paralysis, **Section 9 (Next Best Action)** renders **ONE single primary recommendation** generated by the Recommendation Engine V2 (e.g. `🎯 Complete Economy Revision — Priority: HIGH`). The dashboard strictly avoids presenting multiple competing actions.

## 7. AI Mentor Summary Design
**Section 10 (AI Mentor Executive Summary)** synthesizes outputs across Exam Readiness, Recommendation, and Adaptive Revision into a clean, encouraging executive guidance box (e.g., *"You are steadily improving in the Developing Readiness phase. Focus on Geography Revision today as part of your 30-Day Plan."*).
