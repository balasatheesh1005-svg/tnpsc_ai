# TNPSC Nova AI — Future Roadmap & Long-Term Milestones

---

## Strategic Vision

TNPSC Nova AI aims to be the premier AI-driven learning platform for competitive exam preparation in Tamil Nadu. The long-term architecture focuses on hyper-personalized learning paths, multi-lingual AI tutoring, and enterprise analytics.
